Aerospace / Optimization

Hypersonic aerodynamic modeling & optimization

NASA Ames · Analytical Mechanics AssociatesSummer 2026 · In progress
Outcome
75% less table-generation runtime
Tools & methods
Python / Condor / OpenVSP / CBAERO

The problem

Aerodynamic data are sampled at discrete flight conditions. Shape and trajectory optimization need a fast, differentiable way to estimate the conditions in between, without repeating a high-fidelity analysis at every step.

My contribution

I built and validated a lookup-table process connecting OpenVSP geometry, CBAERO aerodynamic data, and Condor. I extended Condor’s spline Jacobian so sensitivities could pass through the interpolated model and into gradient-based shape and trajectory optimization.

The result

Removing redundant spline construction reduced table-generation runtime by 75%. The lookup model could then be used in the team’s gradient-based CobraMRV optimization loop.
