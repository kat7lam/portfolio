The problem

High-fidelity aerodynamic lookup tables are expensive to generate, while aerodynamic data are only available at discrete conditions across the flyable range. Shape and trajectory optimization therefore needs a fast way to interpolate the rest of the flight envelope without rerunning high-fidelity aerodynamic analysis at every optimization iteration.

My contribution

Built and validated the aerodynamic lookup-table process connecting OpenVSP geometries, CBAERO outputs, and Condor. CBAERO supplied aerodynamic coefficients (CL, CD, CS, CMl, CMm, CMn) at sampled flight conditions; I extended Condor’s spline Jacobian to differentiate the interpolated aerodynamic model with respect to those coefficient-table values so sensitivities could propagate through the lookup table for gradient-based shape and trajectory optimization.

Outcome

Eliminating redundant spline construction reduced aerodynamic table-generation runtime by 75%. By exposing spline sensitivities to Condor, the aerodynamic lookup became usable inside a gradient-based CobraMRV shape-and-trajectory optimization loop, avoiding repeated high-fidelity aerodynamic analysis at every iteration and creating a derivative-based counterpart to the team’s earlier genetic-algorithm process.
