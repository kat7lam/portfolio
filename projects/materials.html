Materials / Applied Research

Compositionally-complex lead-free copper-based alloys

UNSW · Engman Fellowship in Applied Mechanics2025–2026 · Completed
Outcome
Presented at TMS
Tools & methods
Arc melting / SEM / EDS / Optical microscopy / MATLAB

The problem

Graphite could provide self-lubrication in a lead-free copper alloy, but carbon does not readily mix with copper. We investigated how composition and melting sequence affect graphite incorporation.

My contribution

I prepared alloy charges and fabricated samples with an arc melter, varying the composition and melting order. I characterized the samples using SEM, EDS, and optical microscopy, then wrote a MATLAB script to quantify carbon across their cross-sections. Those results guided the next samples.

The result

I presented the research at the TMS Conference. The resulting alloy is being developed for commercial use.
