The problem

Our five-person team assembled a Van’s RV-12. The build brought together mechanical fit, avionics, electrical installation, and careful checks against the technical drawings.

My contribution

I installed avionics, antennas, canopy and latching components, and fasteners. I also read the technical drawings and added and verified lead counterweights for mass balance.

The result

After we completed the build, our professor flew the RV-12. Seeing the finished aircraft take off was the final test of the assembled system.
