The problem

Replace manual barcode-based cylinder tracking with a field-deployable monitoring system that reduces operator intervention.

My contribution

Developing temperature, pressure, location, and tamper sensing while designing the low-power architecture across sensing, wireless, battery, compute, and power-management subsystems. The work includes component trade studies and custom PCB iteration.

Economic modeling & design tradeoffs

I’m applying operations research to compare hardware configurations through a weighted objective. The model weighs initial cost, maintenance, power consumption, and RPN-based risk penalties, so a lower purchase price can be considered alongside the work and risk involved in keeping the system running.

The trade studies include component weight, the number of batteries and magnets, and the support box’s mounting and fit within the UF6 cylinder skirt. These choices connect the economic model to the physical hardware: a configuration has to make sense in both the objective and the available mounting space.

In standard failure mode and effects analysis, the Risk Priority Number (RPN) is the product of severity, occurrence, and detection ratings. I use RPN-based penalties to include risk in the tradeoff analysis. I’m continuing to refine the model as the hardware design develops.

Method reference: NASA FMECA handbook
