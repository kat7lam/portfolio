Low-power UF6 cylinder monitoring hardware

General Matter · HMC Clinic2026–2027 · In progress
Outcome
Hardware in development
Tools & methods
System architecture / Sensor integration / PCB design

The problem

Manual barcode tracking requires operators to check cylinders in the field. Our Clinic team is developing monitoring hardware that combines sensing, wireless communication, and low-power operation.

My contribution

I’m developing temperature, pressure, location, and tamper sensing, along with the power architecture for the sensors, wireless communication, battery, and compute hardware. My work includes component comparisons and custom PCB iteration.

The result

The hardware is in development. Current work focuses on integrating the subsystems and balancing reliability, environmental constraints, and power consumption.
