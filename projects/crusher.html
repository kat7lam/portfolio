CrushCore automated sample-preparation device

CrushCore · Harvey Mudd College2025 · Completed
Outcome
24-sample batch capacity
Tools & methods
CAD / FEA / 3D printing / Testing

The problem

Preparing fly samples by hand takes about 5–10 minutes per sample. The repeated motion is tiring and can lead to inconsistent crushing, while the samples need to stay on ice for mitochondria extraction.

My contribution

I worked on client requirements, design alternatives, CAD, simulation, fabrication, and prototype testing. Our team paired a linear actuator with custom 3D-printed parts to automate the repeated crushing motion.

The result

The device handled 24 samples at once. Mechanical testing and live lab trials showed uniform crushing while preserving sample integrity, and the final prototype was approved for lab use.
