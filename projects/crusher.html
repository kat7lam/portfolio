The problem

The problem with crushing flies by hand is that it takes about 5–10 minutes per sample. People’s hands get tired, so the consistency can be different between samples. The flies need to be crushed to extract their mitochondria for research, and the samples have to stay on ice throughout preparation. Our device speeds that process up while making the crushing more repeatable.

My contribution

Worked through client requirements, design alternatives, CAD, simulation, prototype iterations, fabrication, and testing. The team combined a linear actuator with custom 3D-printed components to automate the repetitive task.

Outcome

The device processed 24 samples in a batch. Mechanical testing and live lab trials demonstrated uniform crushing while preserving sample integrity; the final prototype was approved for lab use.
