Mechanical Design / Medical Robotics

Automated ECMO cannulation system

Lifemotion Medical Technology · HMC Clinic2025–2026 · Completed
Outcome
Ex vivo cannulation trial
Tools & methods
CAD / Machining / MATLAB / Linear actuation

The problem

ECMO supports circulation and oxygenation when the heart or lungs cannot. Placing the cannula is a demanding manual procedure. Our team developed a device to make positioning and insertion more repeatable.

My contribution

I designed and iterated the device’s stabilizing structures, dilator clamp, and linear positioning hardware. I also wrote MATLAB image processing to identify vessels in ultrasound images by circularity and mark their centroids for positioning.

The result

We demonstrated open cannulation of a sheep artery in an ex vivo trial. A patent application was filed, and the design is moving through industrial design toward a more deployable device.
