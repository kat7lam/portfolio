Hobbies & campus life

Hobbies

A matcha pop-up out of my dorm window

I opened a matcha pop-up outside my balcony every other week. It stems from my love of matcha, but I also wanted to get experience just going out and doing things.

What I loved about it was the community that it brought out. A lot of my friends came, but they also brought their friends. People were in line talking with each other and taking photos of the matcha and the stickers I made to go along with them. I hand-drew and printed the stickers, and every time I would have a different menu.

It was a huge success, and it was just really fun to do on the side. Really fun to just see everybody coming out.

Cheer

I’ve been on the cheer team for two years. We’re the inaugural team at the 5Cs, cheering for all five Claremont Colleges.

We learn and perform new dances and stunts at games and alumni events. It is the most amazing feeling ever when we nail a new skill. Everybody is cheering and high-fiving each other for the accomplishment!

Class President

I’ve been Class President from my sophomore through senior year. Part of what I do is coordinate with the other class presidents across the five colleges to create class activities for all five, but also collaborate with individual colleges.

We’ve done matcha and study, registration events where seniors give advice to underclassmen, screen printing our own Class of ’27 merch, and “Pitch Your Single Friend,” which was a hit.
