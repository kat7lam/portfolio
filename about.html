A little
about me

I’m a senior Engineering major at Harvey Mudd College. My work spans controls, optimization, hardware, materials, and experimental research.

I’ve worked on aerospace optimization at NASA Ames, medical devices, embedded systems, and materials research. I like learning how an unfamiliar system works, then getting into the details of building or testing it.

As Class President, I plan events and work with students, faculty, and staff at Harvey Mudd and across the Claremont colleges. I enjoy getting to know people and working with different personalities.

Outside school, I love traveling and exploring new places independently.
