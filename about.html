Hi, I’m Kate

A little
about me

I’m a senior Engineering major at Harvey Mudd College, graduating in May 2027, with a concentration in Economics and an emphasis in Data Science. I’m especially interested in applied mathematics, control theory, and dynamics.

My work has ranged from aerospace optimization at NASA Ames to medical devices, embedded systems, materials research, and molecular engineering. I like learning unfamiliar systems quickly, moving between technical areas, and understanding how the different pieces of a problem fit together.

Outside of technical work, I spend a lot of time involved in campus life. As Class President, I plan events and work with students, faculty, and staff across Harvey Mudd as well as the 5C Claremont Consortium. I enjoy meeting people and communicating across different groups, and being able to build relationships and work well with different personalities is something I value a lot.

Outside of school, I love traveling and exploring new places independently. I’m happiest doing work that is technically interesting, collaborative, and gives me room to keep learning.
